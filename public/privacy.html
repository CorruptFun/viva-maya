Privacy Policy

Viva Maya · Last updated July 28, 2026

Viva Maya is a free match-3 game made by CorruptFun LLC. This policy explains, in plain language, what the game stores and why. The short version: we keep what's needed to save your game progress, plus anonymous notes on which levels are too hard; we don't sell or share any of it; and there are no ads and no third-party trackers.

What the game stores

  • Your game progress — levels completed, stars, best scores, in-game chips, lives, streaks, and your settings. This is saved on your own device.
  • If you sign in with Google (optional): your email address and a unique account ID. These are used only to save your game progress to the cloud so it syncs across your devices and survives clearing your browser or getting a new phone. Google shares your basic profile (name and email) with us when you choose to sign in; we do not receive your Google password.
  • Anonymous gameplay events. So we can see which levels are too hard and what's worth fixing, the game records simple events — the app being opened, a level started, won, failed or quit, and which buttons get used. Each event carries a random ID that we create on your device. It isn't your name, your email, or anything about your phone, and it can't be traced back to you. You can turn this off at any time in Settings → Cloud & Backup.
  • If you turn on the weekly race reminder (optional): the notification address your browser generates for this device, so we can send that one reminder. Turning reminders off deletes it.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, and no third-party ad or analytics trackers. The gameplay events described above are our own — they go straight to our database and are never shared with an analytics company or anyone else.
  • We never sell, rent, or share your information with anyone.
  • We don't build a profile of you, track you across other websites, or collect anything beyond what's listed above. In-game chips are for play only — they have no cash value and can't be purchased or cashed out.

Where it's stored

Game progress lives on your device. If you sign in, a copy is also stored in our database (hosted by Supabase), protected by security rules so that only you can read or change your own saved game — no other player can access it.

Your choices

  • Play without signing in — the game works fully offline, with progress kept only on your device.
  • Sign out any time from Settings → Cloud & Backup.
  • Delete your data — clear the game's data in your browser to remove the local copy, and email us to have your cloud save deleted.
  • Back up your progress to a file any time from Settings → Cloud & Backup.
  • Turn off anonymous gameplay events from Settings → Cloud & Backup. The game plays exactly the same either way.
  • Turn off the weekly race reminder from the same place, or in your browser's notification settings.

Children & families

Viva Maya is a family-friendly game. We only ever store game progress, the anonymous gameplay events described above and — if you sign in — the sign-in email. There is no advertising, no cross-site tracking, and no profile of any player.
